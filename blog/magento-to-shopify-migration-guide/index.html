Magento to Shopify Migration: The Complete Guide to a Successful Shopify Migration from Magento

By Abhishek Porwal July 24, 2026 8 min read

If you're considering a Magento to Shopify migration, you're not alone. As ecommerce businesses grow, many merchants discover that maintaining a Magento store requires significant technical resources, ongoing security updates, hosting management, and developer support. While Magento is a powerful ecommerce platform, it can become expensive and complex to manage over time.

Shopify offers a modern, fully hosted ecommerce platform that simplifies store management while providing excellent performance, security, scalability, and an intuitive admin experience. Whether you're running Magento Open Source or Adobe Commerce, making the move to Shopify can help reduce operational costs while improving your customers' shopping experience.

In this guide, you'll learn everything you need to know about Shopify migration from Magento, including what data can be migrated, how to protect your SEO rankings, and why Shift It Now is the ideal migration solution for your business.

Why Migrate

Why Migrate from Magento to Shopify?

Magento has long been a popular ecommerce platform, but it comes with ongoing maintenance requirements that many growing businesses no longer want to manage.

Businesses choose Magento to Shopify migration for several reasons:

  • Lower maintenance costs
  • Fully managed cloud hosting
  • Faster website performance
  • Automatic platform updates
  • Enterprise-grade security
  • PCI-compliant checkout
  • Extensive Shopify App Store ecosystem
  • Easier store management for non-technical teams

Migrating to Shopify allows your team to spend less time maintaining infrastructure and more time growing sales and improving customer experience.

Migration Steps

How to Migrate from Magento to Shopify

Step 1: Audit Your Existing Magento Store

Before migrating, review your Magento store carefully.

Identify:

  • Active products
  • High-performing categories
  • Customer accounts
  • Historical orders
  • CMS pages
  • Blog content
  • Existing SEO metadata
  • Custom URL structure

Cleaning unnecessary content before migration creates a faster, more organized Shopify store.

Step 2: Set Up Your Shopify Store

Create your Shopify store and configure:

  • Store information
  • Payment gateways
  • Shipping rules
  • Tax settings
  • Store currency
  • Theme selection

Preparing your Shopify store before importing data ensures a smoother migration process.

Step 4: Verify Your Imported Data

After migration, carefully review every section of your Shopify store.

Verify:

  • Product information
  • Product variants
  • Images
  • Pricing
  • Inventory
  • Customer accounts
  • Orders
  • Collections
  • Navigation menus
  • Content pages

Testing before launch helps ensure your customers experience a seamless transition.

Step 5: Protect Your SEO During Migration

SEO preservation is one of the most important parts of a successful Shopify migration from Magento.

Magento and Shopify use different URL structures, making proper redirect mapping essential. Shopify recommends creating 301 redirects for all important pages, verifying redirects after launch, and reviewing metadata on key pages to preserve organic visibility.

To maintain your rankings:

  • Create 301 redirects for all existing URLs
  • Preserve page titles and meta descriptions
  • Keep important product and category pages optimized
  • Submit your updated XML sitemap to Google Search Console
  • Update internal links
  • Monitor indexing and crawl errors after launch

A well-planned SEO migration helps protect years of organic growth and minimizes ranking fluctuations.
