Shopify vs Squarespace: Which Wins for Ecommerce?

By Abhishek PorwalNovember 18, 20266 min read

Shopify vs Squarespace for ecommerce: compare catalog limits, payments, apps, and international selling, plus how to migrate to Shopify when you outgrow Squarespace.

Shopify vs Squarespace comes down to what you're building. Short answer: Shopify wins for ecommerce stores built to grow, while Squarespace wins for design-led sites with small, curated catalogs. If selling products is the core of your business, Shopify's catalog capacity, payment options, and app ecosystem give it a clear edge. If your shop is a side of a portfolio or service business, Squarespace can be the better fit. Below we compare both, show the signs you've outgrown Squarespace, and explain how an ecommerce platform migration to Shopify works when it's time to move.

Where Shopify Wins for Ecommerce

Catalog capacity. Shopify has no cap on product count. Squarespace's documented limits are far lower, and growing stores tend to reach them sooner than expected.

Payments and checkout. Shopify connects to more than 100 payment providers, which matters when you sell in new regions or need a specific gateway.

Apps and sales channels. Subscriptions, reviews, loyalty, and marketplace selling are a few clicks away through the Shopify App Store.

Operations. Multi-location inventory and a built-in point-of-sale system support stores that sell in more than one place.

International selling. Squarespace added multi-currency checkout in a 2026 update, though it leaves out subscriptions and digital products. Shopify Markets goes further, managing currency, languages, domains, and pricing by market.

Where Squarespace Still Wins

Squarespace is excellent for brand-first sites. Its templates are polished, the editor is simple, and it blends content, bookings, and a small shop in one place. If you sell a few dozen products, ship low volumes, and treat design as your main differentiator, you may never need more.

Which Should You Choose?

Choose Shopify if:

  • Selling products is your main business
  • You expect your catalog, orders, or markets to grow
  • You want marketplace, subscription, or advanced inventory options

Choose Squarespace if:

  • Your brand and design matter more than commerce depth
  • You sell a small, curated range
  • Your shop supports a service or content business

Signs You've Outgrown Squarespace

  • You're approaching product or variant limits
  • Customers ask for payment methods you can't offer
  • You want to sell on marketplaces or run advanced inventory
  • Your marketing needs apps that Squarespace doesn't have
  • You're expanding into new countries

What Migration to Shopify Involves

A Shopify store migration from Squarespace moves your data, not your design. A proper Shopify data migration carries products and variants, images, customer accounts, orders, blog posts, and pages. Your design gets rebuilt in a Shopify theme, a good chance to modernize it, and customers sign in with an emailed code instead of a password.

The Shopify migration process runs in four stages: connect your store, preview a demo migration, run the full migration while your store stays live, then launch with a final sync and domain switch. Every old URL also needs a 301 redirect to protect your SEO. Any ecommerce migration is only as good as the data it carries across, so check what moves before you commit. Shopify Migration FAQ: Shopify vs Squarespace

Is Shopify better than Squarespace for ecommerce?

For product-focused stores that plan to grow, yes. Squarespace suits design-led brands with small catalogs.

Can I migrate to Shopify without losing SEO?

Yes, when every old URL gets its own 301 redirect and your titles, descriptions, and alt text are carried across.

What data moves in a Squarespace to Shopify migration?

Products, variants, images, customers, orders, blog posts, and pages. Design does not transfer, and passwords cannot move between platforms.

How long does a Shopify store migration take?

The data transfer typically takes a couple of hours to three days, and the full project one to three weeks including theme setup and testing.

Do most migration tools move everything?

No. Most migration tools stop at products, so confirm yours also covers customers, orders, redirects, and rollback.

Shopify vs Squarespace is not about which platform is better in general. It is about which one fits your catalog, your markets, and your plans. When the answer is Shopify, the right migration tool keeps the move low risk.
