Wix to Shopify: The Complete Guide to Migrating Your Store the Right Way

By Abhishek Porwal July 24, 2026 7 min read

If you built your store on Wix and you're bumping into its ceiling, thin app selection, checkout that can't flex with growth, and no real point-of-sale story, you're not alone. Thousands of merchants make the move from Wix to Shopify every year, and for good reason. Shopify's app ecosystem, POS, and infrastructure are built for stores that are scaling.

But knowing you should migrate your Wix store to Shopify and actually doing it without losing products, customers, orders, or your hard-won Google rankings are two very different problems. Here's exactly what a Wix to Shopify migration involves, what usually goes wrong, and how to do it without the downtime horror stories you've probably read about.

Why Migrate

Why Merchants Are Moving From Wix to Shopify

Wix is a solid starting point, but it wasn't built to be an ecommerce engine first. Shopify offers a deeper app marketplace for shipping, subscriptions, and marketing; a proper POS for merchants who sell in person; and checkout infrastructure that holds up under real traffic spikes. Store owners running wholesale or B2B lines also find Shopify's tooling far more mature.

None of that matters, though, if the migration itself wipes out two years of customer history and blog SEO in the process.

Thinking about the switch? Run a free demo migration with Shift It Now on your real Wix catalog before you commit to anything.

What Moves Over

What Actually Moves When You Migrate a Wix Store to Shopify

A proper Wix to Shopify migration should carry over products and variants, product images, customer accounts and addresses, full order history with line items, blog posts, and site pages.

What typically doesn't transfer, regardless of tool, is anything tied to Wix-specific features with no Shopify equivalent, including Wix Bookings and Events, member-gated content, and restaurant menu modules.

Knowing this upfront means no surprises on launch day.

Migration Steps

The Right Way to Run a Wix to Shopify Migration

A clean migration follows four stages:

  1. Connect your Wix store. OAuth authorization takes about two minutes, with no CSV exports.
  2. Preview a demo migration on a slice of your catalog to check field mapping.
  3. Migrate the full catalog while your Wix store keeps taking orders. This typically takes two hours to three days depending on catalog size.
  4. Launch with a final delta sync, the DNS switch, and ideally a rollback window in case anything looks off.

Talk to a migration expert about your catalog size and timeline before you schedule the move.

SEO Protection

Protecting Your SEO During the Move

Rankings are usually the biggest fear merchants have about migrating. A migration that skips SEO handling will cost you months of recovery time.

Look for these essentials:

  • Automatic 301 redirects on every URL
  • Migrated meta titles and descriptions
  • Preserved image alt text

That combination is what keeps your existing search visibility intact instead of starting from zero on Shopify.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. How long does a Wix to Shopify migration take?

Most stores complete the full migration in two hours to three days, depending on catalog size. A small catalog under 500 products often finishes the same day.

2. Will I lose my SEO rankings when I migrate my Wix store to Shopify?

Not if the tool handles redirects properly. Auto-generated 301 redirects, migrated meta titles and descriptions, and preserved alt text are what keep your rankings intact after the switch.

3. Can I migrate customers and order history from Wix to Shopify, or just products?

This is exactly where most migration tools fall short. Many only migrate products and leave customer accounts and order history behind. A complete migration tool should move customer accounts, addresses, and full order history with line items alongside your product catalog.

4. Do I need a developer to migrate Wix to Shopify?

No, not for a standard Wix Stores migration. OAuth-based connections mean you authorize access in one screen rather than exporting CSVs or writing scripts. Developer help is really only needed for heavily customized or headless setups.

5. What's the difference between a generic migration app and a full-service one?

Generic migration tools typically move only sample data or products, leaving you to manually re-enter customers, rebuild order history, and set up your own redirects. Full-service tools, like Shift It Now, migrate the complete store including products, customers, orders, blog content, and SEO redirects in one pass, with a rollback safety net if something looks wrong after launch.

6. What doesn't migrate from Wix to Shopify?

Features with no direct Shopify equivalent generally don't transfer. This includes Wix Bookings and Events, member-gated content, and restaurant menu modules.
